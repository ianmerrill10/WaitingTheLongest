# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is Waiting The Longest™?

Waiting The Longest™ is a platform dedicated to highlighting shelter animals who have been waiting the longest for adoption. We aggregate data from shelters across the country to bring visibility to animals that might otherwise be overlooked.

### Why focus on animals waiting the longest?

Animals that have been in shelters for extended periods often get passed over for newer arrivals. By highlighting them, we hope to give them the visibility they deserve and help them find their forever homes.

### Is this a shelter or rescue organization?

No, we are not a shelter. We're a technology platform that aggregates and displays data from partner shelters and rescue organizations. All adoptions go through the individual shelters.

## Adoption Questions

### How do I adopt an animal I see on this site?

Click on the animal's profile to find information about the shelter they're at. Each animal's page includes a link to the shelter's website or contact information. The adoption process is handled directly by the shelter.

### Can I adopt if I live far from the shelter?

This depends on the individual shelter's policies. Many shelters do offer transport or work with rescue networks. Contact the shelter directly to ask about their policies.

### Are the animals shown actually available?

We update our data regularly, but shelter situations can change quickly. Always contact the shelter directly to confirm an animal is still available before making plans.

### Why might an animal have been waiting so long?

There are many reasons an animal might have a longer shelter stay:
- They may be a senior pet
- They might be a "less popular" breed
- They could have special needs (medical or behavioral)
- They might be overlooked due to their color (black dog syndrome)
- Some are bonded pairs that need to be adopted together
- The shelter might be in a less populated area

None of these reasons make them less deserving of love!

## Technical Questions

### How often is the data updated?

We update our data every 6 hours from partner shelters. Some shelters update more frequently than others.

### Which shelters do you include?

We partner with shelters that provide data through RescueGroups, Petfinder, and other animal welfare networks. If your shelter would like to be included, please contact us.

### The wait time seems wrong for an animal. Why?

Wait times are calculated from the intake date provided by the shelter. Sometimes shelters may have animals that were previously adopted and returned, or animals transferred from other facilities. The intake date reflects when they entered the current shelter.

### Can I save my favorite animals?

Yes! Use the heart icon on any animal's card to save them to your favorites. Your favorites are stored in your browser's local storage and will persist between visits.

### Does the site work offline?

We have basic PWA (Progressive Web App) support, so some features may work offline. However, you'll need an internet connection to see the latest animal listings.

## For Shelters

### How can my shelter be included?

We aggregate data from animal welfare networks. Ensure your shelter is listed on platforms like RescueGroups or Petfinder, and your animals will automatically be included.

### Can we customize how our animals appear?

We display the data as provided through the APIs. For the best presentation:
- Include clear, high-quality photos
- Write detailed, engaging descriptions
- Keep intake dates accurate
- Update animal status promptly when adopted

### My animal was adopted but still shows on your site

Our data updates every 6 hours. If an animal shows as available longer than that, please ensure their status is updated in your shelter management system and the API data has been refreshed.
