Frequently Asked Questions

How do I adopt an animal from your site?

We display animals from various shelters and rescues. When you find a pet you're interested in, click their profile to see the shelter's contact information. You'll work directly with the shelter for the adoption process.

Is there an adoption fee?

Adoption fees vary by shelter. We don't collect any fees – all payments go directly to the shelter or rescue organization handling the adoption.

How do you determine "days waiting"?

The "days waiting" count is based on when the animal was first listed as available for adoption, as reported by the shelter or rescue organization.

Can I list my pet for adoption?

We only list animals from verified shelters and rescue organizations. If you need to rehome a pet, please contact a local shelter or rescue for assistance.

How can I help if I can't adopt?

You can share animal profiles on social media, consider fostering through a local shelter, donate to rescue organizations, or volunteer your time. Every action helps!
