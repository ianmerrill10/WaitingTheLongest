About

Waiting The Longest™ helps shelter animals who have waited the longest get seen first.

We pull adoption listings from rescue and shelter sources, rank them by time waiting, and make it easy to share long-waiting pets with friends and family.

Because every day matters.

Resources

Adoption prep checklist
What to do before bringing a pet home
  • Verify landlord / housing rules
  • Find a local vet and schedule a first visit
  • Get food, bowls, bed, and a safe crate/carrier
  • Pet-proof your home and yard
Shelter support
Ways to help if you can’t adopt
  • Foster (even short term helps)
  • Share long-waiting pets on social media
  • Donate supplies or volunteer time
  • Sponsor adoption fees
