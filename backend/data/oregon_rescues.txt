To assist with building a database, the following tables list every known animal rescue in Oregon – including government-run shelters, private non-profits, wildlife rehabilitators, and farm sanctuaries – along with their contact information and a brief description. Breeders, pet stores, or for-profit sellers are excluded, as requested.
